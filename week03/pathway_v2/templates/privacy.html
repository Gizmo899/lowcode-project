YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

A clear overview of the personal information used in this proof-of-concept service.

What this PoC stores

Pathway stores your account email address and password hash. For jobseekers it also stores your name, phone number, assigned adviser, CV versions, job applications, review comments and in-app notifications.

Why it is used

The information supports the career-support service: managing your account, sharing your CV with your assigned adviser, tracking job applications and delivering in-app reminders and weekly summaries.

Who can see it

You can see your own records. Your assigned career adviser can see your contact details, CV versions and application records. Other jobseekers and other advisers cannot access your information through the application.

Retention and deletion

The PoC provides account closure for jobseekers and removes active records and private CV files. Backup expiry and any legal retention exceptions require an organisation-level policy before production use.

Your rights and contact

The organisation operating this service must provide a full privacy notice, identify and document the lawful basis for processing, and establish processes for access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection where applicable and data portability. This PoC notice is not a substitute for that work.

Do not upload real personal data to a test deployment. The organisation must review its legal basis, retention policy, privacy notice and security controls before processing real jobseeker information.
