YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

A clear overview of the personal information used in this service.

What is stored

Your name, email address and a scrambled version of your password (never the password itself). For jobseekers also an optional phone number, the career adviser you chose, your CVs (the files themselves), your job applications including any notes, follow-up reminders and in-app notifications.

Why it is used

To provide the service you signed up for: managing your account, sharing your CVs with your adviser for approval, tracking your job applications and giving you in-app reminders and a weekly digest. No emails are sent and no data is shared with anyone else.

Who can see it

You can see all of your own information. The career adviser you chose can see your name, email address, CVs, and job applications including your notes, and can approve or reject your CVs. Your adviser cannot see your phone number. Other advisers and other jobseekers cannot see your information. Whoever runs the computer where the service is hosted can technically reach the stored data.

How long it is kept

Your information is kept until you close your account. Closing it deletes your profile, CVs, applications, reminders and notifications at once. Read notifications and finished reminders are also removed automatically after 90 days. Backups expire after a fixed period.

Your rights and contact

You can see, correct and delete your information in the service. You can ask the organisation that runs it about restriction, objection or a copy of your data, and you can complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland. The organisation must confirm its lawful basis, retention periods and contact details before real data is used.

Do not upload real personal data to a test deployment. Please do not include ID numbers, photos or health details in a CV.
